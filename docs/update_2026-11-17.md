## 3. Results

**Baseline reproduction (RQ1).** PCA PC1+PC2 = **99.55 %** (paper: 99.62 %). The MLP with 10 hidden units, as in the paper:

| Variant | LOO | 10×5-fold |
|---|---|---|
| Literal (ΔG = Gmax − Gmin, mean-centring, SGD) | 51.7 % | 54.7 % |
| Best (ΔG/Gmin, mean-centring, SGD) | **83.3 %** | 79.1 ± 3.1 % |
| Rodríguez et al. 2010 (reported; 16 meas./4 classes, 35/9) | 100 / 92.5 % | n/a |

**Experiment A: fusion of the 8 sensors in S1 (RQ2).** Low-level: curves → PCA → LR, **91.7 ± 2.1 %** (LOO 93.1 %). Mid-level: 120 features → SVM, 86.7 ± 2.2 %. High-level: per-sensor SVM → stacking, 84.3 ± 2.9 %. Low beats mid and high (Wilcoxon p = 0.002); mid vs high is not significant.

**Ablations (RQ5) change how this ranking should be read:**
- The odour reaches the sensors at 32–34 s in *every* recording, yet the first 30 s alone give the low-level model **85.5 %**.
- Baseline-normalised signals cost it 16–18 points.
- On level-free (z-scored) signals, **mid-level features are best (85.9–87.2 %)**.

Much of the low-level advantage is therefore the sensors' pre-sample state (session or drift), not aroma. Also:
- LOO-selected winners are about 4 points optimistic against repeated CV;
- no single feature type matters;
- sliding windows don't help the CNN.

**Experiment B: S1 + SmellNet (RQ3), a negative result.** Pooled training lowers coffee accuracy by 5–17 points compared with identical S1-only models (p ≤ 0.008 at mid level). Decision-level fusion only recovers the S1-only level (77.9 vs 77.2 %). AB8 shows that even 10 % of SmellNet hurts as much as all of it, so the cause is sensor mismatch, not data volume. Removing the polarity correction or the per-recording normalisation makes the loss much larger (up to −38 points).

**Experiment C: CQI groups → quality tertile (RQ4).** Non-sensory data is weak and the fusion level doesn't matter: C1 49.6 %, C2 48.1 %, C3 49.8 % (chance 33 %; p ≥ 0.06). Origin is the most useful group. Low grades are marked by cup defects (about 3× more category-one defects), which is what the e-nose targets.

**Experiment D: sensor + GC-MS in SmellNet (RQ6), sub-experiment.**
- Giving the model the true class's GC-MS vector yields **100 %** (leakage); the same model with label-free GC-MS drops to 5.2 %.
- Leak-free contrastive training beats sensor-only for the MLP in all 4 settings (+0.9 to +1.9 points; the paper reports +2.2 to +6.5).
- CNN/LSTM/Transformer (about 6 GPU-hours) were postponed (question 3).
